# Privacy

Metronome collects no data and makes no network connections. It does not declare the
`INTERNET` permission, so Android will not let it connect to anything.

- **Microphone (tuner only).** When the tuner is open and you have allowed microphone
  access, the app reads sound from the microphone and measures its pitch in memory, a
  fraction of a second at a time. The audio is overwritten as it goes. It is never
  recorded, saved to storage or sent anywhere. The microphone is released as soon as you
  leave the tuner or the app goes to the background.
- **Settings and presets.** Your tempo, metre, accents, presets, A4 calibration and
  instrument choice are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own
  device backup may include them if you have backup turned on. They are removed when you
  uninstall the app.
- **No analytics, crash reporting, ads, accounts or third-party SDKs.**
